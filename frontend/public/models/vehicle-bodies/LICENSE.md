Original, unbranded models by Innerscene, released under **CC0 1.0 Universal**.
Free to use, modify and redistribute, including commercially.

- License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Library terms: https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/terms
- Original model pages, download URLs and SHA-256 checksums: [sources.json](./sources.json)
- Prepared asset checksums: [prepared.json](./prepared.json)

HERMES adaptations: neutral exterior paint for the vehicle's stored color;
unused paint images removed; body-specific orientation, framing and hotspots.
The chassis-cab illustration is derived from the box truck by removing its
freight enclosure and fittings. Other materials and geometry are preserved.

The garage uses this collection for the box truck, chassis cab and tipper.
The road vehicle illustrations are listed in the [studio license](../studio/LICENSE.md).

No external asset service is contacted when using the application. Prepared GLBs
are bundled with HERMES; normal builds do not download or regenerate models.
