# OpenX vehicle models in HERMES

HERMES uses the original GLBs from [OpenX Assets 20261015](https://github.com/vevalabs/openx-assets/releases/tag/20261015). The [GLB archive](https://github.com/vevalabs/openx-assets/releases/download/20261015/openx-assets-glb.zip) contains the vehicle model source files and their per-file attribution in `REUSE.toml`. The checked-in GLBs are byte-for-byte copies. HERMES scales and repaints the models, and moves their existing panels and light elements at runtime.

The OpenX adaptations are by [Dogan Ulus and OpenX Assets](https://github.com/vevalabs/openx-assets) and carry both [MPL 2.0](MPL-2.0.txt) and [CC BY 4.0](CC-BY-4.0.txt), as stated in the release's `REUSE.toml`.
