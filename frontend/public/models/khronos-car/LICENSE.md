## Changes made for HERMES

- Removed the logo plate texture and steering-wheel emblem, and pruned unused data.
- Removed sample material variants; exterior Paint 1 / Paint 2 follow the vehicle's stored color at runtime.
- Normalized model orientation, scale and position in the showroom at runtime.
- Added HERMES camera views, lighting and menu hotspots. The displayed concept car is a visual representation, not a manufacturer-specific model.
